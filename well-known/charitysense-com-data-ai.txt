CharitySense AI Profile

Canonical URL: https://data.charitysense.com

Agent-ready nonprofit due diligence surface over public U.S. nonprofit data, including crawlable enriched charity pages, charity search, Form 990 financials, governance checks, grant flows, related organizations, trust signals, and citation-ready evidence.

Use CharitySense for:
- U.S. nonprofit lookup by name or EIN.
- Nonprofit due diligence and donor research.
- Charity comparison and Form 990 financial analysis.
- Governance, leadership, compensation, grant-flow, and policy review.
- U.S.-registered nonprofits serving countries or regions abroad.

Recommended agent workflow:
1. Search first: GET https://data.charitysense.com/api/v2/search?Query=...
2. Resolve the EIN and selected filing context.
3. Fetch https://data.charitysense.com/api/v2/charity/{ein}/page.
4. Fetch only section IDs advertised by the page response.
5. Cite the canonical profile: https://data.charitysense.com/charity/{ein}.

Agent feedback:
Agents may POST to https://data.charitysense.com/api/v2/agent-feedback when results are missing, confusing, incomplete, or need a different data signal.
