# CharitySense

> Independent, AI-powered verification for charitable infrastructure. CharitySense provides real-time monitoring that confirms funded hospitals, schools, water wells, and orphanages are operational — giving donors, foundations, NGOs, and governments proof that their investments are working.

## About

CharitySense is built by OsciLabs. It is fully independent — not affiliated with any charity, foundation, or government body. Verification uses AI-powered analysis of on-device sensor data and edge computing, not self-reported metrics.

- **Donors**: Independent verification that funded projects are operational with real-time evidence and transparent impact reporting.
- **Foundations**: Portfolio-wide grant monitoring across multiple charitable infrastructure investments and regions.
- **NGOs & Charities**: Automated donor transparency tools providing verifiable proof of project impact without manual reporting overhead.

## Monitored Verticals

- **Water Wells & Pumps**: Pump activity, estimated usage, user counts, peak times, uptime. Status: deploying.
- **Hospitals & Clinics**: Patient traffic, department utilization, peak hours, staff patterns. Status: live.
- **Schools**: Attendance proxies, classroom utilization, facility usage.
- **Orphanages & Care Facilities**: Activity verification, safety indicators, operational status. Status: live.
- **Construction & Infrastructure**: Progress milestones, activity detection, trend analysis.
- **Agriculture & Farming**: Crop production, yield tracking, water efficiency, land use.

## Key Differentiators

- Fully independent — not affiliated with any charity, foundation, or government body.
- AI-powered analysis of sensor data, not self-reported metrics.
- Privacy-first design: no personal data collection, no facial recognition, no surveillance of beneficiaries.
- All data remains sovereign to the project site with edge-processed analytics.
- Works with intermittent power and connectivity in remote locations.
- On-device processing — no cloud dependency for core analytics.
- Runs on solar. Works on 3G and satellite.

## Technology

- Edge AI computing on existing camera feeds and sensors
- No new infrastructure required at project sites
- Built for intermittent power and connectivity (solar, 3G, satellite)
- On-device processing with full data sovereignty — no video leaves the site
- Motion vectors and selective snapshots, not continuous recording
- Real-time operational dashboards accessible from anywhere
- Powered by OsciLabs Vision
